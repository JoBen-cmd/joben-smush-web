# Resources

Here's a list of resources that will often be used on this website or may be helpful for your future endevaors.

#### Communities
* [Smash Ultimate Modding Hub Discord](discord.gg/ASJyTrZ) - Modding discord for Smash Ultimate.

#### Documents
* [Smash Ultimate Material Research](https://scanmountgoat.github.io/Smush-Material-Research/introduction/index.html) - A great way to view more information about Smash Ultimate's rendering.
* [C#'s Stage Info](https://csharpm7.github.io/stages/) - A webstie that contains niche information for a handful of stages in Smash Ultimate

#### Applications
* [SSBH Editor]
* [UltTex]
* [MSBT Editor]
* [prcEditor]
* [Switch Toolbox]

#### Tutorials
* [A Modder's Guide to Substance Painter](https://gamebanana.com/tuts/19599) - An advanced guide on how to bake meshes & exporting it into Smash's texture formats.

#### 3D Modelling
* [Blender](https://www.blender.org/download/releases/) - It's free and compatible with Smash Ultiamte Blender making it much easier to import/export models.
* [Smash Ultimate Blender](https://www.blender.org/download/releases/) - A Blender plugin for Smash Ultimate models and animations.

#### 2D Texture Editing
* [Photopea](https://photopea.com/) - A free 2D image editing sotware

> [!NOTE]
> Any 2D image editing software such as Photoshop, GIMP, Paint.net, etc. should be fine to use for Smash Ultimate.
